# Circle Up — phone mock-up rules (apply to every screen)

Product: "Circle Up" puts people at an event into small groups of 3–5 so nobody stands alone. People join on a web page from their own phone — no app, no account. User sends one screen at a time.

- Device: phone held in one hand, 375px wide, one column, scrolls down. Show every screen in a phone frame.
- Thumb-friendly: tap targets ≥48px tall, full-width buttons, important actions near the bottom.
- Noisy room: large text, high contrast, short sentences, no tiny grey text, nothing needing zoom.
- English only.
- Look: calm and warm. Teal brand colour, warm off-white background, rounded corners, generous spacing, friendly serif headings (like Fraunces), plain sans-serif body. No clutter. Max one main button per screen.
- Privacy: never show anyone's location (a fixed meeting spot in the room is OK; a person's whereabouts is not). Never show anyone how they were rated, never show who rated whom.
- Made-up content: realistic names/text; every piece of made-up data gets a small visible "test data" tag.
- Empty/waiting states (when asked): second phone frame next to the main one.
- Reply format: the mock-up only, plus one line listing anything unsure about.
